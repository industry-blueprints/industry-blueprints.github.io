<section class="listing"><div class="container">

  <div class="empty-state"><h3>Technology options follow proven patterns</h3><p>Product and technology-category mappings are Phase 4 work in <code>build-order.md</code> — they wait until real solution patterns and implementation patterns exist to map against, so evidence drives the mapping instead of a vendor list driving the architecture.</p></div>
