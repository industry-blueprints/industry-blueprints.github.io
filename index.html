How this is built

Three things that make it different

Maturity is the spine

Advice that assumes a capability you don't have is worse than no advice. Every AI opportunity and pattern declares the level it presupposes, and every playbook is written as a transition between two levels.

Current state gets equal billing

Every process describes how it typically runs today and why it works that way. Current-state dysfunction usually has a reason — a statute, an audit finding, a system that can't do the thing. Blueprints that treat it as incompetence don't survive contact with the people who live it.

Provenance is visible

Every artifact shows who wrote it, whether a human verified it, what it's based on, and when it goes stale. Badges are derived from the metadata, never hand-set. Content nobody stands behind is labeled, not hidden.

Draft library

This is an early, deliberately narrow build: one capability — constituent service and case management — threaded end to end to prove the model, across federal and state/local government. Most artifacts are marked unverified, which is accurate rather than modest.

The maturity rubric in particular is written from research and has not yet been walked through with real organizations. Treat disagreement from one as a defect in the rubric.
