02 · The catalog

Five kinds of blueprint

Each kind answers a different question about what an industry app should contain.

Personas

Who does the work — their goals, permissions, and the app experiences built for them.

Use Cases

The concrete jobs to be done — what triggers work, and what "done" looks like.

Processes

The stages a record moves through, and the checklist of activities inside each stage.

App Experiences

The tailored, role-specific surfaces built on top of a process — dashboards, workbenches, portals.

Agent Skills

What an AI assistant should know and be allowed to do for a given persona and process.
