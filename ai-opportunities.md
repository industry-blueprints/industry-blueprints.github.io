<header class="page-hero"><div class="container"><p class="eyebrow">Cross-cutting view</p><h1>AI Opportunities</h1><p class="lede">AI is an overlay across the six components, not a seventh one. Every opportunity tagged anywhere on the site uses this same controlled taxonomy.</p></div></header>
<div class="container"><div class="prose">

<p>An AI opportunity attaches to a workload, capability, or use case rather than becoming its own place in the hierarchy — the relationship is <code>AUGMENTED_BY</code>. Two controlled vocabularies keep that tagging consistent site-wide: what the AI does, and what role it plays relative to the person doing the work.</p>

<h2>AI pattern — what it does</h2>

<table>
<tr><th>Pattern</th><th>What it does</th><th>Public-sector examples</th></tr>
<tr><td>Summarize</td><td>Condenses large volumes of information.</td><td>Case summary, grant file summary, incident recap</td></tr>
<tr><td>Extract</td><td>Turns unstructured content into structured facts.</td><td>Entities, dates, amounts, locations, document fields</td></tr>
<tr><td>Classify</td><td>Categorizes and routes content or work.</td><td>Complaint routing, evidence classification, request triage</td></tr>
<tr><td>Search &amp; Answer</td><td>Retrieves grounded knowledge and produces answers.</td><td>Policy assistant, case research assistant, staff knowledge search</td></tr>
<tr><td>Generate</td><td>Drafts new content from context.</td><td>Correspondence, reports, narratives, notices</td></tr>
<tr><td>Recommend</td><td>Suggests actions while preserving decision authority.</td><td>Next-best action, maintenance recommendation, case prioritization</td></tr>
<tr><td>Predict</td><td>Estimates future events or outcomes.</td><td>Equipment failure, demand, workload volume, risk</td></tr>
<tr><td>Detect</td><td>Finds anomalies, patterns, or suspicious behavior.</td><td>Fraud signals, unusual transactions, outlier activity</td></tr>
<tr><td>Match / Resolve</td><td>Identifies likely identity or relationship matches.</td><td>Duplicate applicants, related entities, record linkage</td></tr>
<tr><td>Optimize</td><td>Finds better allocation, routing, scheduling, or resource choices.</td><td>Fleet sizing, inspector routing, staffing allocation</td></tr>
<tr><td>Vision / Perception</td><td>Interprets images, video, or sensor information.</td><td>Damage assessment, infrastructure defects, document image analysis</td></tr>
<tr><td>Automate / Agentic</td><td>Executes bounded multi-step work using tools and controls.</td><td>Collect records, update case, request missing data, prepare package for review</td></tr>
</table>

<h2>AI role — how it relates to human authority</h2>

<table>
<tr><th>Role</th><th>Mental model</th><th>Example</th></tr>
<tr><td>Assistant</td><td>Helps a person perform work faster.</td><td>Summarize a case file.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Analyst</td><td>Finds patterns, predictions, or insights.</td><td>Identify anomalous fleet fuel usage.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Advisor</td><td>Recommends an action while a human decides.</td><td>Prioritize inspections using risk indicators.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Automator</td><td>Performs bounded repeatable work automatically.</td><td>Classify and route a request.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Agent</td><td>Coordinates multiple steps and tools toward a goal.</td><td>Gather records, check completeness, request missing information, prepare package for review.</td></tr>
</table>

<h2>Qualifying an opportunity</h2>

<p>Before an AI opportunity gets promoted into a Use Case's Readiness lens, it should be tested against every one of these dimensions — not just the exciting ones.</p>

<table>
<tr><th>Dimension</th><th>Question</th></tr>
<tr><td>Business Value</td><td>How meaningful is the operational or public-service benefit?</td></tr>
<tr><td>Data Readiness</td><td>Is sufficient, representative, authorized data available?</td></tr>
<tr><td>Technical Feasibility</td><td>Can current technology perform the task reliably enough?</td></tr>
<tr><td>Process Readiness</td><td>Is the underlying process sufficiently defined and stable?</td></tr>
<tr><td>Human Oversight</td><td>Where must a person review, decide, approve, or be able to override?</td></tr>
<tr><td>Decision Impact</td><td>What is the consequence of error or inappropriate automation?</td></tr>
<tr><td>Explainability</td><td>Must the recommendation or output be understandable and defensible?</td></tr>
<tr><td>Privacy / Security</td><td>What sensitive, regulated, or restricted information is involved?</td></tr>
<tr><td>Automation Potential</td><td>Assistive, partially automated, or highly automated?</td></tr>
<tr><td>Time to Value</td><td>How quickly can useful value be demonstrated?</td></tr>
<tr><td>Monitoring / Evaluation</td><td>How will quality, drift, failure, and misuse be detected?</td></tr>
</table>
