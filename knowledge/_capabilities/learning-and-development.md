## Purpose

To ensure people have the knowledge and certification their role requires, develop capability the
organization will need, and prepare successors for the roles it is about to lose.

**Compliance training and capability development are different activities sharing a budget line.**
The first must be completed and evidenced; the second must change what someone can do. Managing
both to a completion metric guarantees the second is not achieved.

## Desired outcomes

- Mandatory and role-required training completed within its window, and evidenced
- Certifications tracked with expiry, and renewal prompted before lapse
- Development aligned to capability the organization actually needs
- Learning available at the moment of need, not only in scheduled events
- Successors identified and prepared for roles at retirement risk
- Effect measured on performance, not on attendance

## Key processes

Training requirement identification by role · curriculum and pathway design · scheduling and
delivery · completion tracking and evidence · certification and licence tracking with renewal
prompts · continuing education credit management · tuition assistance administration · development
planning · leadership and supervisory preparation · succession identification and readiness ·
effectiveness evaluation

## Key data

Requirement matrix by role and by regulation · completion records with dates and evidence ·
certification register with issuer, number, and expiry · development plans linked to
[position](/capabilities/position-and-classification-management/) requirements · course catalogue
with outcomes · succession candidates and readiness assessments · skills inventory ·

## Measures

| Measure | Class |
|---|---|
| Mandatory training completed within its required window | Process |
| Certifications current, and lapses in the period | Process |
| Renewal prompted before expiry rather than after | Process |
| Critical roles with an identified, ready successor | Process |
| Internal promotion rate into supervisory roles | Outcome |
| Capability change demonstrated after development | Outcome |
| Training spend against identified capability gaps | Input |

**Successor readiness for critical roles is the measure that matters most**, because the
demographic exposure in government is unusually concentrated — and it is almost never reported.

## Level variance

- **Federal.** Extensive mandated training with government-wide requirements, formal leadership
  development pipelines, and structured executive development programmes.
- **State.** Central learning platforms serving agencies, with substantial licensure-driven
  continuing education in health, social care, and public safety.
- **County / municipal.** Public safety certification with statutory hour requirements and
  recertification cycles, alongside very limited development budget for everyone else. Small
  jurisdictions frequently rely on state-provided or association training, which sets the
  effective curriculum.

## Where it goes wrong

**Completion as the measure of everything.** Ninety-eight percent completion of a module nobody
remembers. It satisfies the requirement and demonstrates nothing.

**Certification expiry unwatched.** Captured at hire, never monitored, and the lapse found during
an inspection — with the work performed in the interim now in question.

**Development as a reward.** Conference attendance allocated to the well-regarded rather than to
where the capability gap is.

**Succession as a list.** Names against roles, no development plan, no readiness assessment, and no
conversation with the named person, who frequently does not want the job.

**Training substituting for design.** A process that requires people to remember twelve exceptions
does not need better training; it needs fewer exceptions. Compare [grounded knowledge
retrieval](/patterns/grounded-knowledge-retrieval/), which addresses the same problem at the moment
of need.

**Knowledge and training run separately.** The knowledge base has the current answer, the course
has last year's, and staff are told to consult both.

**Retirement exposure known and unaddressed.** Projections produced annually, filed, and the
capability loss absorbed as a surprise when it arrives.
