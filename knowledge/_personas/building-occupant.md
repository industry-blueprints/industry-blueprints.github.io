Also: Staff Occupant, Tenant Agency Employee. Distinct from a
[constituent](/personas/constituent/) — this is someone who works *in* the building rather than
someone visiting it for a service, though the two overlap at the front counter.

## Role summary

Shows up, works, and notices the building the way anyone notices infrastructure — only when it
stops working. Reports what's wrong, requests what's needed, and has no say in whether either gets
addressed.

## Goals

- Report a problem once, to one place, and know what happens to it
- Get a comfortable, safe, working space without having to negotiate for it
- Not have to know who's actually responsible for fixing something
- Trust that a locked door, a broken sensor, or a safety concern gets treated as urgent

## Pain points

**No idea where a request goes.** Email a name, call a number, or wait for a friend in facilities
to notice — none of it comes with a way to check status.

**Requests judged by relationships, not need.** The person who knows the facilities manager gets a
response today. Everyone else assumes nothing is happening, and often nothing is.

**Small problems left to compound.** A minor leak or a failing sensor reported once, unaddressed,
and reported again weeks later as a bigger and more expensive problem.

**No visibility into why something takes so long.** A request that requires a permit, a part on
order, or a contractor's schedule looks identical, from the occupant's side, to one that was simply
ignored.

**Safety concerns treated the same as comfort requests.** "It's cold in here" and "I smell gas" can
enter through the same unstructured channel with no way to tell them apart on arrival.

## What this persona needs from a blueprint

A single, visible way to report anything — comfort, safety, access, or a straightforward
request — with a status they can check without calling anyone. And a channel that treats "I smell
gas" differently from "my chair is broken" the moment it's reported, not after someone reads it.

## Typical questions

- Did anyone get my request, and is it actually being worked on?
- Why has this taken three weeks when the last one took a day?
- Who do I even ask about this?
- Is this actually dangerous, or is it just annoying?
