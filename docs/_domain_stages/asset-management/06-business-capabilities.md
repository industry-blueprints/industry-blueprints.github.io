The domain capability model identifies the abilities that collectively make Asset Management effective. It gives leaders and practitioners a stable way to discuss strengths, gaps, ownership, information, and investment without organizing the conversation around the current application landscape.

Capability boundaries are intentionally broader than individual processes or teams. Work Management, for example, includes the ability to plan, schedule, authorize, perform, complete, and learn from work across the portfolio.
