Asset information becomes useful when people can connect a stable asset identity to its location, purpose, condition, work, cost, risk, and planned future. That does not require every detail to live in one system; it requires clear ownership and dependable relationships among authoritative sources.

Information quality is best understood in relation to use. Planning a field visit, authorizing work, forecasting renewal, reporting financial value, and comparing investments each need different levels of completeness, currency, precision, and history.
