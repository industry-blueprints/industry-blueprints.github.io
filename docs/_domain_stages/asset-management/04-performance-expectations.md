Performance measures connect daily asset activity to service and stewardship outcomes. Agencies often combine service measures, portfolio health, information quality, maintenance performance, capital delivery, and long-term affordability rather than relying on one condition score.

Each measure needs a clear purpose, definition, population, timing, source, and owner. A small set used in recurring decisions is usually more educational and useful than a large dashboard without an operating rhythm.
