Analytics can help agencies understand portfolio health, compare needs, forecast work and funding, and test investment scenarios. AI may add value where the work is information-intensive or pattern-oriented—for example, helping an inspector review images or helping a planner locate relevant maintenance history.

Each candidate is considered separately. Its value, information readiness, consequence of error, human role, controls, and measures are part of the use case itself. The presence of an AI technique does not determine whether a use case belongs in operations.
