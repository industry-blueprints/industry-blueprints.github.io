Public assets are a means of delivering service. A road supports safe travel, a facility supports the programs inside it, and a water system supports health and daily life. Starting with those public outcomes gives asset decisions a purpose that remains understandable even when budgets, projects, or technologies change.

At this stage, teams describe the services the portfolio enables and the outcomes that matter most. That shared direction later helps them interpret condition, risk, cost, and investment choices in context.
