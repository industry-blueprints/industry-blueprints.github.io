Asset Management becomes durable when it is part of regular agency decisions rather than a one-time planning effort. Governance identifies who maintains shared definitions, reviews portfolio performance, approves investment principles, resolves cross-functional questions, and sponsors improvement.

Continuous improvement uses service outcomes, measures, operational experience, audits, incidents, project learning, and community feedback to update priorities. The same discipline applies to analytical and AI-enabled practices, including ongoing evaluation and clear decisions to adjust or retire them.
