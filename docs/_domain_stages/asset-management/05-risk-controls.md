Criticality helps an agency distinguish assets whose performance has a greater effect on service, safety, compliance, cost, or community well-being. Condition and criticality are related but different: an asset can be in poor condition without being highly critical, or highly critical while currently in good condition.

Controls make important stewardship practices dependable. Examples include assessment cycles, work authorization, project handover checks, transparent investment criteria, retained decision records, and defined review or escalation paths.
