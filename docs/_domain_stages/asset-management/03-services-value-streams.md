The asset lifecycle describes how assets move from planning and creation through operation, care, renewal, and retirement. Value streams connect that lifecycle to the people and teams who plan investments, deliver projects, operate services, inspect condition, complete maintenance, and update records.

Following the work end to end makes handoffs visible. It also shows where the same information—such as asset identity, location, condition, service contribution, cost, and work history—needs to remain coherent across organizational boundaries.
