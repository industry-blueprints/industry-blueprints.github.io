Asset Management looks different across a transit authority, water utility, county facilities office, and parks department. A useful scope identifies which assets and services are included, who owns and operates them, and which decisions the effort is intended to improve.

The operating context also records practical boundaries such as jurisdiction, funding sources, regulatory obligations, climate and geography, workforce capacity, contracting arrangements, and relationships with other public bodies.
