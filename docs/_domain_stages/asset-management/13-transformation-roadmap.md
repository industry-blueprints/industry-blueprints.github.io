The roadmap turns priorities into a sequence that an agency can fund, deliver, absorb, and learn from. It accounts for dependencies such as establishing asset identity before connecting systems, defining condition methods before comparing portfolio results, and clarifying decision rights before automating handoffs.

Useful roadmaps connect delivery waves to operational outcomes and learning. They make assumptions visible and create deliberate points where leaders can confirm, adjust, accelerate, or stop planned work.
