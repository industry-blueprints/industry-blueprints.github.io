Asset decisions span organizational boundaries. Field teams understand operating conditions, engineers interpret condition and treatment choices, service owners understand public impact, finance teams understand affordability, and executives balance priorities across the portfolio.

Process and decision mapping shows how these perspectives come together. It records the information available at each decision, who recommends, who decides, who contributes expertise, and how outcomes return to the asset record and future planning.
