Asset Management is how a public agency develops a dependable understanding of its physical assets and coordinates their care over time. It connects what the agency owns or operates to location, condition, service purpose, maintenance history, risk, cost, and lifecycle plans.

The Domain applies across facilities, roads, bridges, water systems, vehicles, park assets, equipment, and mixed portfolios. Specific standards and assessment methods vary, while the central stewardship questions remain familiar.
