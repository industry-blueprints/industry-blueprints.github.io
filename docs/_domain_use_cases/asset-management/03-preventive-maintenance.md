---
id: preventive-maintenance
title: Plan Preventive Maintenance
description: Turn asset needs, strategies, and operating constraints into coordinated work that preserves service and useful life.
domain_id: asset-management
data_key: asset_management
domain_url: /domains/asset-management/
order: 3
permalink: /domains/asset-management/use-cases/preventive-maintenance/
outcomes:
  - Important maintenance is planned and completed at useful intervals.
  - Field teams receive clear scope, asset history, safety information, materials, and access details.
  - Completed work improves the asset record and future maintenance plan.
people:
  - Maintenance planner
  - Service worker or field technician
  - Crew supervisor
  - Asset manager
  - Operations and scheduling staff
information_ids: [asset, location, condition, inspection, work-order, maintenance-history, risk]
measure_ids: [planned-reactive-work, service-reliability]
stage_ids: [services-value-streams, business-capabilities, processes-roles-decisions, applications-integration, performance-expectations]
flow:
  - Define maintenance strategies using asset class, manufacturer guidance, condition, criticality, and operating experience.
  - Forecast upcoming work and coordinate labor, contractors, materials, access, outages, and seasonal constraints.
  - Authorize and schedule work with a clear asset, scope, priority, and completion expectation.
  - Complete the work and record observations, actions, labor, materials, cost, and follow-up needs.
  - Review recurring findings and adjust intervals, strategies, asset plans, or renewal needs.
architecture: >-
  EAM or CMMS commonly coordinates maintenance strategy, work orders, schedules, history, labor,
  and materials while mobile tools support field execution. Connections to GIS, inventory,
  procurement, finance, documents, and operational systems reduce duplicate entry at handoffs.
ai: >-
  Retrieval and summarization can help planners find relevant history, manuals, and recurring
  observations. Recommendations remain grounded in source records and maintenance standards, with
  planners retaining responsibility for the work scope and schedule.
---

Preventive maintenance translates stewardship plans into recurring operational action. Its value comes not only from scheduling work, but from completing the feedback loop between the plan, field experience, asset condition, cost, and future decisions.
