An asset inventory is more than a list. It is the common reference that connects physical things to their location, ownership, service purpose, condition, work history, cost, documents, and future plans.

Agencies can build trust incrementally by defining the information needed for real decisions, starting with important asset classes, and making updates part of normal operational work.
