---
id: project-handover
title: Coordinate Project-to-Operations Handover
description: Bring new or renewed assets into service with the identity, documents, ownership, maintenance, financial, and operational information teams need.
domain_id: asset-management
data_key: asset_management
domain_url: /domains/asset-management/
order: 4
permalink: /domains/asset-management/use-cases/project-handover/
outcomes:
  - Operations teams can safely use and care for an asset when it enters service.
  - Required asset, warranty, maintenance, document, location, and financial records are complete and connected.
  - Project acceptance and operational readiness are understood together.
people:
  - Project manager
  - Asset manager
  - Operational owner
  - Engineer or inspector
  - Maintenance planner
  - Records, GIS, finance, and technology staff
information_ids: [asset, asset-class, location, condition, inspection, cost, project-investment]
measure_ids: [handover-completeness, inventory-coverage]
stage_ids: [services-value-streams, processes-roles-decisions, information-data, applications-integration, governance-improvement]
flow:
  - Define information and operational-readiness requirements while the project is being planned.
  - Prepare asset records, hierarchy, location, documents, warranties, parts, maintenance plans, and training before acceptance.
  - Review the physical asset and its information with the receiving operational owner.
  - Register or update authoritative records and connect project, financial, spatial, document, and maintenance information.
  - Confirm open items, ownership, warranty responsibilities, and the transition into normal operation and maintenance.
architecture: >-
  Handover crosses project, document, asset, spatial, financial, and maintenance systems. Shared
  identifiers, structured deliverables, validation rules, and observable workflow help information
  move into operational records without treating a document package as the final asset record.
ai: >-
  Extraction and completeness assistance may help teams compare submitted documents with agreed
  handover requirements. The receiving owner confirms completeness and operational readiness;
  automated extraction does not constitute acceptance.
---

Project handover is the point where a delivered asset becomes an operating responsibility. Treating information and readiness as part of acceptance helps preserve project knowledge and gives operating teams a stronger beginning.
