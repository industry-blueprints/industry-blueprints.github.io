---
id: condition-criticality
title: Assess Condition & Criticality
description: Develop a current, consistent understanding of asset condition and the importance of each asset to public service.
domain_id: asset-management
data_key: asset_management
domain_url: /domains/asset-management/
order: 2
permalink: /domains/asset-management/use-cases/condition-criticality/
outcomes:
  - Condition information is current enough to guide maintenance, renewal, and service decisions.
  - Critical assets receive attention consistent with their service and community importance.
  - Assessment results can be compared across time and similar assets.
people:
  - Inspector or engineer
  - Asset manager
  - Service or program owner
  - Maintenance planner
  - Risk, safety, or compliance specialist
information_ids: [asset, asset-class, location, condition, inspection, risk, service-level]
measure_ids: [condition-currency, service-reliability, lifecycle-plan-coverage]
stage_ids: [performance-expectations, risk-controls, processes-roles-decisions, information-data, analytics-ai]
flow:
  - Define assessment methods and cycles appropriate to each asset class and level of criticality.
  - Prepare inspection work using asset identity, location, history, access, and safety information.
  - Observe and record condition, defects, evidence, method, date, and assessor.
  - Review significant findings and connect them to service, safety, cost, and community consequence.
  - Use the result to inform maintenance, monitoring, lifecycle strategy, and investment planning.
architecture: >-
  Mobile field tools, GIS, asset records, document storage, and work management commonly support
  this use case. The architecture preserves the observation, source evidence, assessment method,
  date, asset identity, and any reviewed condition rating as connected but distinguishable records.
ai: >-
  Image or sensor analysis can help organize observations and flag material for closer review.
  Inspectors confirm observations that become official condition records, and performance is
  evaluated across relevant asset classes and operating conditions.
---

Condition describes the asset’s present physical state. Criticality describes how much its performance matters. Considering them together helps an agency direct assessment, maintenance, monitoring, and renewal attention where it contributes most to public service.
