---
id: portfolio-performance
title: Monitor Portfolio & Service Performance
description: Use a focused set of measures to understand asset health, service contribution, plan delivery, information quality, and emerging needs.
domain_id: asset-management
data_key: asset_management
domain_url: /domains/asset-management/
order: 7
permalink: /domains/asset-management/use-cases/portfolio-performance/
outcomes:
  - Teams share a current view of portfolio health and its relationship to public service.
  - Measures inform recurring operational, planning, investment, and governance decisions.
  - Trends and material changes lead to focused learning and improvement.
people:
  - Asset and maintenance managers
  - Service and program owners
  - Performance and analytics staff
  - Finance and capital planning staff
  - Executives and oversight bodies
information_ids: [asset, condition, inspection, work-order, maintenance-history, cost, risk, service-level, project-investment]
measure_ids: [inventory-coverage, condition-currency, service-reliability, lifecycle-plan-coverage, renewal-funding-alignment, planned-reactive-work]
stage_ids: [mission-outcomes, performance-expectations, analytics-ai, governance-improvement]
flow:
  - Select measures tied to important service, stewardship, operational, information, and investment decisions.
  - Define each measure’s purpose, population, method, source, owner, timing, and interpretation.
  - Produce and review the measures at a cadence suited to the decisions they inform.
  - Explore material changes with operational context rather than treating the dashboard as the conclusion.
  - Record actions, monitor their effects, and refine measures as the capability develops.
architecture: >-
  Performance views commonly integrate service, asset, inspection, work, cost, project, and spatial
  information. Shared definitions, lineage, refresh timing, access, and drill-through to source
  context help readers interpret results consistently.
ai: >-
  Analytics and anomaly detection can help identify patterns or changes worth investigating.
  People interpret those signals in operational context, and measures track whether any AI-enabled
  intervention actually improves the intended service or stewardship outcome.
---

Portfolio performance is most useful as a recurring conversation about service and stewardship. Measures provide evidence for that conversation; they do not replace the experience of operators, engineers, communities, or decision-makers.
