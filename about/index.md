<section class="section">
  <div class="wrap about-grid">
    <article><h2>Domain-centered</h2><p>The main experience begins with a public-sector mission or use case. Supporting concepts are introduced in context and remain available in the Reference Library.</p></article>
    <article><h2>Vendor-neutral</h2><p>The work, information, decisions, and outcomes are described independently of a particular application or platform. Technology examples remain clearly identified as examples.</p></article>
    <article><h2>Educational and practical</h2><p>The material uses plain language and connects concepts to recognizable work, people, information, measures, workshops, and planning outputs.</p></article>
    <article><h2>Designed for reuse</h2><p>Common framework concepts are defined once and applied across domains. This allows courses, assessments, architecture views, and modernization tools to share one coherent foundation.</p></article>
