## Overview

To allocate finite public money across obligations that exceed it, on a statutory timetable, in
a way the adopting body will approve and the public can scrutinize.

## Why it matters in the public sector

This capability supports responsible stewardship of public money and clear explanations of how resources are raised, allocated, and used. It gives leaders and staff a shared way to coordinate the work, make decisions visible, and understand whether it is producing the intended result.

## Personas

- Budget Director
- Agency or Department Director
- Elected Official

## Common processes

1. Budget call and instructions
2. revenue forecasting
3. departmental request preparation
4. request review and analysis
5. executive recommendation
6. public hearing and deliberation
7. adoption
8. initial appropriation load

## Outcomes and measures

- A balanced, adopted budget by the statutory deadline
- Allocations that reflect stated priorities rather than last year plus an increment
- Revenue forecasts accurate enough that mid-year correction is not routine
- Departments able to see the consequence of what they were given
- A public document someone outside finance can actually read
