## Overview

To define what conduct is permitted for people exercising public authority, collect the
disclosures that make conflicts visible, and investigate allegations to a standard that holds.

**The operative test is usually appearance, not corruption.** Most ethics failures in government
involve no personal gain — they involve a relationship that was not disclosed, and a decision that
consequently cannot be defended.

## Why it matters in the public sector

This capability helps a public agency exercise authority openly, lawfully, and accountably. It gives leaders and staff a shared way to coordinate the work, make decisions visible, and understand whether it is producing the intended result.

## Personas

- Internal Auditor
- Human Resources Director
- Elected Official

## Common processes

1. Standards issuance and training
2. financial disclosure filing and review
3. conflict declaration against specific matters
4. recusal management and screening
5. gift and hospitality registration
6. outside employment and post-employment review
7. advice requests and opinions
8. allegation intake and triage
9. investigation
10. determination and sanction
11. reporter protection

## Outcomes and measures

- Disclosure obligations known to those who carry them, before they need to
- Conflicts declared against actual matters rather than in the abstract
- Recusal that is effective, not merely recorded
- Advice available before the decision, and known to be available
- Allegations investigated promptly, impartially, and to a consistent standard
- Reporters protected, and visibly so

| Measure | Class |
|---|---|
| Disclosure filings complete and on time | Process |
| Filings actually reviewed rather than only collected | Process |
| Conflicts declared against specific matters | Process |
| Recusals with an effective screen in place | Process |
| Time from allegation to determination | Process |
| Advice requests received — a rising number is a good sign | Output |
| Recurrence of the same breach type | Outcome |

**Advice requests are the counter-intuitive measure.** More of them means people know the rules
exist and where the line is. An organization with no advice requests does not have no problems.
