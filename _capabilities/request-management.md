## Overview

Request Management enables an organization to turn an expressed need into accountable work. A
request may ask for information, a service, a decision, a correction, or referral to another
organization. It begins when the organization accepts the need for handling, regardless of the
channel through which it arrived.

The capability produces four outcomes: the requester knows the request was received; the
need is classified consistently; responsibility is explicit; and the disposition is recorded.

It does not require every request to become a Case. A simple question may be answered immediately.
One interaction may contain multiple requests, while a request requiring extended coordination may
open or join a case.

## Why it matters in the public sector

This capability shapes how people and organizations experience public services, requests, and decisions. It gives leaders and staff a shared way to coordinate the work, make decisions visible, and understand whether it is producing the intended result.

## Personas

- Service Seeker
- Intake Worker
- Service Coordinator
- Service Worker
- Service Supervisor

## Common processes

1. Receive and acknowledge the request.
2. Classify the need, service, topic, urgency, and possible duplication.
3. Route the request to a responsible team or make a traceable referral.
4. Perform the requested service, provide the answer, or coordinate related work.
5. Communicate useful status and any information still needed.
6. Record the resolution, referral, withdrawal, or other disposition.
7. Close, reopen, or relate the request to another request or case when needed.

## Outcomes and measures

| Desired outcome | Useful measures |
|---|---|
| The requester knows the request was received | Acknowledgment time and coverage |
| Consistent classification | Classification agreement and correction rate |
| Clear responsibility | Misrouting rate; requests without a responsible owner |
| Timely handling | Time to first action and resolution; aging requests |
| An understandable disposition | Reopened requests; resolution completeness; requester feedback |
