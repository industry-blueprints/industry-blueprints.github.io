## Overview

To provide recreation programmes, operate recreational facilities, and steward parks and open space
— delivering the programming, registration, reservations, and access that make them usable.

**Distinct from the physical asset.** Maintaining the fields, buildings, and trails is
maintenance management and
asset management; this capability is the service delivered
through them. Confusing the two is why parks departments are frequently funded as a maintenance
line and measured on acreage rather than on use.

## Why it matters in the public sector

This capability shapes how people and organizations experience public services, requests, and decisions. It gives leaders and staff a shared way to coordinate the work, make decisions visible, and understand whether it is producing the intended result.

## Personas

- Constituent
- Program Manager

## Common processes

1. Programme planning and scheduling
2. instructor and seasonal staff recruitment and certification
3. registration, waitlisting, and payment
4. fee setting, scholarship, and waiver administration
5. facility and field reservation and permitting
6. special event permitting
7. aquatics operation and lifeguard certification
8. youth and senior programme delivery
9. out-of-school-time and summer programming
10. sports league administration
11. park ranger and site operations
12. volunteer and friends-group coordination
13. concession and partnership management
14. open space and trail stewardship
15. park planning and level-of-service analysis
16. community needs assessment

## Outcomes and measures

- Park and facility access distributed so that proximity does not depend on income
- Programming matched to community demand rather than to historical schedule
- Registration easy, and pricing that does not exclude
- Fee assistance available without a process that deters applying
- Facilities and programmes accessible to people with disabilities, in practice
- Reservations and permits handled predictably, including for community use
- Youth programming aligned with the hours families actually need
- Usage measured, including by whom

**Walkable park access by neighbourhood is the measure that reframes this capability.** Total
acreage is the number usually reported and it says nothing about who can reach a park; the
distribution is a legacy of historical investment patterns and is measurable in an afternoon with
existing spatial data.
