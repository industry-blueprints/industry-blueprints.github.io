## Overview

To ensure money is spent only for authorized purposes, within authorized amounts, in the
authorized period — and to make that provable afterwards.

## Why it matters in the public sector

This capability supports responsible stewardship of public money and clear explanations of how resources are raised, allocated, and used. It gives leaders and staff a shared way to coordinate the work, make decisions visible, and understand whether it is producing the intended result.

## Personas

- Budget Director
- Program Manager
- Internal Auditor

## Common processes

1. Appropriation load and allotment
2. encumbrance and commitment control
3. expenditure processing
4. budget transfer and amendment
5. variance monitoring and reporting
6. position control
7. year-end close and carryforward

## Outcomes and measures

- No overspend against appropriation
- No material underspend either, which represents service not delivered
- Encumbrances that give an accurate picture of remaining spending power
- Variance visible early enough to act on
- Spending traceable to its authority

level of granularity, not that execution is undisciplined.
