## Investigations

**Workload:** Investigations
Managing the end-to-end process of receiving allegations or leads, establishing facts, collecting and analyzing information and evidence, documenting findings, and referring or resolving matters in support of law enforcement, regulatory, administrative, program-integrity, internal-affairs, and other public-sector missions.

**Representative workload variants:** criminal investigations, fraud and program-integrity investigations, regulatory investigations, internal affairs, administrative investigations, background/suitability investigations, financial investigations, and inspector general investigations.

> **Use-case notation:** **[Core]** = conventional business/technology use case · **[AI]** = AI-enabled or AI-augmented use case

### A few particularly reusable capabilities

For the knowledge model, I would resist making every capability investigations-specific. Several above should probably become **canonical capabilities shared with other workloads**:

| Canonical capability              | Other workloads that could reuse it                                     |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Intake & Triage**               | Benefits, Grants, Licensing, Complaints, FOIA                           |
| **Case Management**               | Benefits, Child Welfare, Legal, Regulatory Enforcement                  |
| **Entity Management**             | Licensing, Grants, Benefits, Regulatory Compliance                      |
| **Document / Records Collection** | Audits, Grants, Legal, Inspections                                      |
| **Interview Management**          | Investigations, HR, Child Welfare, Equal Employment                     |
| **Evidence Management**           | Investigations, Inspections, Enforcement, Public Safety                 |
| **Financial Analysis**            | Investigations, Grants, Revenue, Procurement                            |
| **Field Operations**              | Investigations, Inspections, Emergency Management, Public Works         |
| **Reporting**                     | Almost every workload                                                   |
| **Referral Management**           | Investigations, Benefits Integrity, Inspections, Regulatory Enforcement |

That gives you a strong distinction between a **workload-specific composition** and the **canonical capability library** underneath it.

For example:

> **Investigations**
> = Intake & Triage + Case Management + Entity Management + Records Collection + Interviews + Evidence + Analysis + Reporting + Referral + Oversight

while:

> **Regulatory Inspections**
> = Planning + Scheduling + Field Operations + Evidence + Compliance Assessment + Corrective Action + Reporting

The capabilities become the reusable building blocks; the workload is the composition of those blocks. That's exactly the behavior we want from the framework.
